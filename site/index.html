GTA 6 tools, with the evidence

A short list of GTA VI tools, one sentence on what each does, and what we could check about it. Kept small on purpose.

Read this first. We have not checked any of these against genuine GTA VI files. What a tool can do is what its author says. We inspect the source and release files, and that is all we claim. How we check

Why this exists

I'm William. I wrote a spec and a small toolkit for the .ttpf texture format, and while doing it I found a lot of other people's work with no easy way to tell what was solid and what was a lure. A tool only gets listed here when its licence, history and author can be checked. More will appear as projects age. The crawler and its rules are in the repo.

Something wrong or missing? Open an issue on the repo or email tidydesksoftware@outlook.com.
